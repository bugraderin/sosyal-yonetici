Sosyal Yönetici

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Sosyal Yönetici is a desktop application designed to run locally on your computer. This policy describes information the Application handles when you connect accounts and manage content.

Information handled on your device

The Application may store social account identifiers and profile details returned by a platform, account language preferences, drafts, captions, media file paths, schedules, application settings, and platform access credentials. These records are stored in the Application’s local database. Access credentials are encrypted using Electron’s operating-system secure storage when that facility is available; the Application refuses to save a credential if encryption is unavailable.

Information sent to platforms

When you connect an account, request analytics, or publish content, the Application sends the information needed for that action directly to the selected platform’s official API. This may include an authorization token, account or media identifiers, captions, selected media, and publication settings. The platform then processes that information under its own privacy policy. The Application does not operate a cloud account or server that receives your tokens or content.

Local AI features

If you enable the optional Ollama integration, text you submit for local suggestions is sent to the Ollama service running at the address configured on your own device. The Application does not send that text to an Application-operated AI server.

Sharing, tracking, and retention

The Application does not operate an advertising or analytics service and does not intentionally send your local database to its developer. Your records remain on your device until you remove them or delete the Application’s local data. Removing an account from the Application deletes its locally stored credential; you can also revoke access in the connected platform’s settings.

Security

Keep your computer account protected and do not share access tokens or screenshots that reveal them. No local storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. The Application cannot recover or delete information held by a third-party platform; manage that information through the platform directly.

Children and policy changes

The Application is not designed for children. This policy may be updated as the Application’s features change. The effective date above will show the date of the latest revision.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions about the Application, visit the project contact page: GitHub profile. Do not post access tokens, passwords, or other sensitive account information publicly.
